### Client Challenge
A major Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) in the Indian energy sector was facing increasing operational costs due to unplanned drilling incidents. Specifically, stuck pipe events were occurring at a rate of 1 per 5 deep-water wells, resulting in millions of dollars in Non-Productive Time (NPT).

### The Solution: TENETX Deployment
Dpulseai deployed the TENETX platform—a physics-native AI system designed to operate on-premise at the rig site. 

**Key Technical Pillars:**
- **Sensor Ingestion:** Integrated directly with rig-side WITSML streams.
- **Physics-Informed Modeling:** Utilized hydraulic pressure equations to constrain neural network outputs.
- **Air-Gapped Deployment:** Ensured 100% data sovereignty within the PSU's private infrastructure.

### The Methodology
1.  **Phase 1: Discovery:** Analysis of historical drilling data to identify pre-incident signatures.
2.  **Phase 2: Edge Integration:** Installation of low-latency compute units at the rig.
3.  **Phase 3: Real-Time Monitoring:** 24/7 automated analysis with uncertainty-bound predictions.

### Operational Outcomes
After a 6-month pilot across 4 offshore rigs, the system delivered measurable ROI:

- **22% Reduction in NPT:** Successful early detection of 4 major stuck pipe risks.
- **Sub-Second Inference:** Predictions delivered faster than human operator reaction time.
- **₹8.2 Crore Saved:** Estimated savings in recovered rig-time and equipment protection.

> "TENETX didn't just give us data; it gave us actionable foresight grounded in engineering reality."

### Infrastructure Stack
- **Compute:** Local Edge Server (8-core ARM architecture)
- **Model:** TENETFlow (8B Parameters)
- **Latency:** <100ms for critical anomaly detection
